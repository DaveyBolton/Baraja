Blue/Ice = control, Green/Money = draw/economy, Purple/Royalty =
utility/command, Gold/Gem = late-game power, Silver/Death = execute, plus
the specials (Candle, Marigold Bomb, Sealed Grave, Copal Smoke) each anchor
one signature card.
