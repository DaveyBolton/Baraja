# Baraja de los Muertos — rules, briefly

For anyone who hasn't played this genre before (Slay the Spire, Balatro, etc.):

1. **Your deck is your toolkit.** No board, no board pieces — each fight is a
   1-on-1 card battle between you and one enemy.
2. **A turn is: draw a hand, spend energy, play cards, end turn.** Cards are
   attacks (deal damage), skills (block, draw, utility), or powers (a
   standing effect for the rest of the fight).
3. **The enemy telegraphs its next move.** You always see what it's about to
   do before you act — no hidden information, no reflexes required.
4. **Win a fight, pick a card.** After each win, choose 1 of 3 new cards to
   permanently add to your deck. This is the core hook: your deck gets
   stronger and more specialized as the run goes on.
5. **Fights chain into a run.** A run is a fixed sequence of fights (8-10
   regular + 1 boss in v1), each harder than the last.
6. **Losing ends the run.** You start the next run with a fresh starting
   deck — that's the "roguelike" part. There's no permanent progression in
   v1; the replay value comes from different card offers each run.

The skill ceiling is in deckbuilding: picking cards that combo with what
you already have, rather than just picking whichever number is biggest.
